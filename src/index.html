No heating or hot water? Try these three things first

Half the call-outs we get in the first cold week are one of these. They take five minutes and could save you the visit.

  1. Look at the pressure

    Most combi boilers stop below 1 bar. Open the filling loop under the boiler slowly until the needle reaches 1.2, then close it. If it drops again within a week, book us in.

  2. Check the thermostat and timer

    Flat batteries in a wireless thermostat are the most common fault of all. Turn it up 3 degrees above the room temperature and check the timer is on.

  3. Thaw the condensate pipe

    In a frost the white plastic pipe outside can freeze and the boiler locks out. Pour warm (not boiling) water along it, then press reset on the boiler.

Still cold? Book a repair or call 07700 900300. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the National Gas Emergency line on 0800 111 999.
